📖 NCERT Context

In NCERT Class 12 Biology (Chapter 12), RNAi is described as a natural cellular defence mechanism. Scientists introduced nematode-specific genes into tobacco plants in a way that produces double-stranded RNA (dsRNA). When Meloidogyne incognita nematodes feed on the transgenic tobacco roots, they ingest this dsRNA. Inside the nematode, the dsRNA triggers RNAi — silencing the nematode's own genes — causing death of the parasite and protecting the plant.

🦠 The Pest

Meloidogyne incognita is a root-knot nematode that parasitises tobacco, causing severe crop loss.

✂️ Dicer Enzyme

Cleaves dsRNA into 21–23 bp fragments called siRNA (small interfering RNA).

🔇 RISC Complex

RNA-Induced Silencing Complex unwinds siRNA and uses it as a guide to find & degrade complementary mRNA.

🌿 Result

Nematode genes are silenced → parasite dies → tobacco plant survives without chemical pesticides.

🧪 dsRNA Source

The dsRNA is produced from both sense & antisense strands of the introduced nematode-specific gene.

🛡️ Why It's Safe

The siRNA is highly specific — only targets nematode gene sequences, not plant or human genes.

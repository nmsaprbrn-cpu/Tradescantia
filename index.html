📌 Quick Reference

Important terms & concepts

Mendelian Laws

Law of Dominance, Law of Segregation, Law of Independent Assortment — basis of inheritance patterns.

Central Dogma

DNA → RNA → Protein. Replication, Transcription, and Translation are the core processes.

Darwinian Fitness

Survival of the fittest — organisms best adapted to environment have higher reproductive success.

Recombinant DNA

Combining DNA from two different sources using restriction enzymes and ligases to create GMOs.

Ecological Pyramid

Graphical representation of number, biomass, or energy at each trophic level in a food chain.

Biodiversity Hotspots

Regions with exceptionally high species diversity and endemism, facing significant habitat loss threats.
